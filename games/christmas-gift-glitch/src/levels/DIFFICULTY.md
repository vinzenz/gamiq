# Difficulty

The Christmas campaign keeps the tested TOTS difficulty data without changes. It uses the same
board masks, spawn weights, goals, move budgets, star limits, boss health, boss throw timing, and
obstacle placement for all 40 levels.

The curve has these stages:

- Levels 1 to 5 teach matching and all four power-ups.
- Levels 6 to 12 teach power-up combinations.
- Chapters add tinsel, parcel piles, frost, ribbon locks, and spreading paper one at a time.
- A boss closes each chapter. The last chapter has boss fights at levels 37 and 40.
- Boss health rises from 6 to 16. Throw timing falls from every four moves to every three moves.

This copy is deliberate. It lets visual theme and story vary while the shared engine and level
curve stay comparable. If a future play test changes one campaign's difficulty, record the reason
in this file before changing the level data.
